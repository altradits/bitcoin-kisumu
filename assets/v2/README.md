# Logo concepts v2 — land, culture & learning

Brief: a creative logo with **no Bitcoin symbol** and **definitely no lake imagery**
(no waves, water, sunset-over-the-lake, or boat). Open `preview.html` in a browser
to see all three side by side with size and dark-background tests.

## The concepts

### A. Kit Mikayi Dawn (`concept-a-kit-mikayi.svg`)
The balancing rocks of Kit Mikayi — the landmark every local recognises — with
the sun rising behind and an open book as the foundation. Knowledge is what
everything balances on. Two birds mark it as a dawn on land.
Feeling: heritage, permanence, trust. Best for schools, partners, institutions.

### B. The Learning Tree (`concept-b-learning-tree.svg`)
A flat-top acacia the community gathers under. The trunk is a learner with
raised arms, the roots feed an open book, gold fruits are ripening ideas.
Feeling: warmth, growth, togetherness. Best for cohorts and youth programs.

### C. Dala K (`concept-c-dala-k.svg`)
A bold geometric **K**. The upper arm reaches up to hold the sun of knowledge;
the lower leg is a clay path grounded in home soil — *dala* is Dholuo for home.
Simplest silhouette; survives favicons, stickers, and embroidery.
Feeling: modern, confident. Best everyday mark.
