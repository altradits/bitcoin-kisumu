# Community Introduction — 250–300 words (send to Arnold)

Why: We started Bitcoin Kisumu because financial exclusion is still normal around Lake Victoria — high fees, slow settlement, and savings that melt to inflation. Bitcoin, learned properly, is a practical alternative, and nobody was teaching it here in an open, beginner-friendly way.

Where: We operate in Kisumu, Kenya's lakeside city, serving students, traders, fishermen, boda riders, and small businesses across Kisumu County and the wider western Kenya region.

How: We educate the My First Bitcoin way — free, open-source, in-person first. Cohorts work through the Bitcoin Diploma material with trained educators, hands-on wallet setup, and Lightning practice. No hype, no trading promises, just fundamentals: money, Bitcoin, security, and self-custody.

What: We run monthly BitDevs-style meetups, beginner wallet workshops, school and campus outreach, a merchant onboarding track for shops that want to accept Bitcoin, and educator training so graduates can teach the next cohort.

When: Right now we are completing our Education Network profile, publishing this website, and recruiting our first full cohort plus two more educators. Next: graduate cohort 1, run a youth holiday program, onboard five local merchants, and publish our first transparency report.

Who: We are trying to reach curious beginners first — youth, students, market traders, and community groups — especially people who have never had a bank account that worked for them. If you live around the lake and want to understand money, karibu Bitcoin Kisumu.
