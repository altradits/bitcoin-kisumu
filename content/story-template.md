# Story Interview Template

Use for every Cohort 1 graduate, newly trained educator, and Lake Victoria
pilot earner/merchant. One interview → one entry in `app/lib/stories.js` →
evidence for the Leaderboard and grant reports.

## Rules

- Record real quotes only. Never invent, polish into fiction, or merge answers.
- Get explicit consent to publish name, photo and quotes (verbal on record is fine).
- Keep answers short: 1–3 sentences each.

## Questions

1. **Background** — Who are you, and what did you do before Bitcoin Kisumu?
2. **First contact** — How did you hear about us, and what made you join?
3. **What you learned** — What is the one thing you can do now that you could not do before?
4. **What changed** — What is different in your daily life or work since graduating/earning?
5. **Livelihood** — Have you earned, saved or spent sats? Give the concrete example.
6. **Advice** — What would you tell someone in Kisumu who is curious but unsure?

## Publishing checklist

- [ ] Consent recorded (name + photo + quotes)
- [ ] Photo saved to `public/stories/<name>.jpg`
- [ ] Entry added to `app/lib/stories.js` with `kind: 'story'`
- [ ] Cohort label correct (e.g. `Cohort 1`, `Educator`, `Dunga pilot`)
- [ ] Reported to the Leaderboard (graduates/educators/earners evidence)
