## Bitcoin Kisumu

**Free, open-source Bitcoin education for Kisumu, Kenya.**

### Problem
Kisumu's households and micro-businesses pay some of the world's highest remittance and payment fees, settle slowly, and save in currencies that lose value. Bitcoin knowledge that could help is locked behind English-only internet content, trading scams, and Nairobi-centric events that never reach the lake region.

### Solution
A local, in-person education community teaching the My First Bitcoin open-source curriculum: the Bitcoin Diploma for students, wallet-and-Lightning workshops for traders, merchant onboarding for shops, and educator training so the work compounds. Graduates feed five Lake Victoria revenue pillars (cold chain, biogas, eco-tourism, remittances, digital exports) so free classes lead to paid livelihoods — see the playbook at /guide.

### Programs
1. **Cohorts** — 8–10 week Bitcoin Diploma groups, free, graduation on-chain/certified.
2. **Meetups** — monthly open BitDevs-style gatherings: talks, demos, Q&A.
3. **Workshops** — first-wallet setup, Lightning, security & self-custody.
4. **Merchant track** — a 10–15 merchant Dunga Beach pilot cluster with B2B suppliers, listed in our public directory.
5. **Educator pipeline** — train 4 local educators in year one.

### Operating model
Volunteer-led, donation-funded, transparent. Funds go to venue, printouts, transport for educators, and subsidised phones/data for students who need them. Community funds sit in a 3-bucket multi-sig treasury (2-of-3 hardware custody, zero single-key control) with quarterly public audits on /treasury.

### Sustainability path
Education → livelihoods → autonomy. The 90-day Dunga Beach pilot targets an earned-revenue ratio ≥25% (earned sats ÷ OpEx); pillar income scales that toward 80–100% by month 12, so grants become a top-up, not the lifeline.

### Year-one goals
- Graduate 50+ students across 3 cohorts
- Train 4 educators (Ivy + 3)
- Launch the 10–15 merchant Dunga Beach cluster
- 12 meetups, 6 workshops
- Reach Tier 3+ in the Education Network (500+ pts)
