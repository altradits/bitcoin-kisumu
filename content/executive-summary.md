## Bitcoin Kisumu

**Free, open-source Bitcoin education for Kisumu, Kenya.**

### Problem
Kisumu's households and micro-businesses pay some of the world's highest remittance and payment fees, settle slowly, and save in currencies that lose value. Bitcoin knowledge that could help is locked behind English-only internet content, trading scams, and Nairobi-centric events that never reach the lake region.

### Solution
A local, in-person education community teaching the My First Bitcoin open-source curriculum: the Bitcoin Diploma for students, wallet-and-Lightning workshops for traders, merchant onboarding for shops, and educator training so the work compounds.

### Programs
1. **Cohorts** — 8–10 week Bitcoin Diploma groups, free, graduation on-chain/certified.
2. **Meetups** — monthly open BitDevs-style gatherings: talks, demos, Q&A.
3. **Workshops** — first-wallet setup, Lightning, security & self-custody.
4. **Merchant track** — help 5+ Kisumu shops accept Bitcoin in year one.
5. **Educator pipeline** — train 4 local educators in year one.

### Operating model
Volunteer-led, donation-funded, transparent. Funds go to venue, printouts, transport for educators, and subsidised phones/data for students who need them. Quarterly transparency reports published on this site.

### Year-one goals
- Graduate 50+ students across 3 cohorts
- Train 4 educators (Ivy + 3)
- Onboard 5 merchants
- 12 meetups, 6 workshops
- Reach Tier 3+ in the Education Network (500+ pts)

### Team

### Ask
Venue partners, printing sponsors, used phones, and Lightning donations to keep every class free.
